# 🔎 Scenario 04 — 5W1H Investigation Record

## 📌 WHO

- **SOC Analyst / Threat Hunter:** Lubaba
- **Resolver-visible client:** `10.50.30.20 / dns-soc-victim01`
- **Defender resolver:** `10.50.30.10 / dns-soc-resolver01`

**Attribution limit:** SOC telemetry identified the resolver-visible client, not the originating process, user, malware, attacker or operator.

## 📌 WHAT

Seven unique, long DNS A queries appeared beneath `tunnel.soclab.abdul4rehman215.tech` within approximately 12 seconds. The behavior independently satisfied all frozen Detection v1.0 conditions. Seven corresponding resolver replies returned `NOERROR`.

The behavior was consistent with structured DNS tunneling-like activity, but defender evidence did not establish payload contents, malware, successful data transfer, compromise or unauthorized intent.

## 📌 WHEN

- First suspicious query: `2026-09-02 16:37:19.215675 UTC`
- Last suspicious query: `2026-09-02 16:37:31.370200 UTC`
- Official alert: `2026-09-02 16:39:01 UTC`
- AI processed: `2026-09-02 16:39:21.727239 UTC`
- Later structurally different namespace activity: `2026-09-02 16:49:50.201643 UTC`
- No second one-minute frozen-pattern match in the reviewed period.

## 📌 WHERE

- Client: `10.50.30.20 / dns-soc-victim01`
- Resolver: `10.50.30.10 / dns-soc-resolver01`
- Parent: `tunnel.soclab.abdul4rehman215.tech`
- Environment: DNSentinel AWS lab
- Primary telemetry: `index=dns_soc_dns host=dns-soc-resolver01 sourcetype=unbound:dns`

Scope was limited to one resolver-visible client, one parent namespace and one matching one-minute window.

## 📌 WHY

Seven fresh long child labels were concentrated under one parent within seconds and met the frozen combined rule. Legitimate long labels existed in the baseline, but no non-Scenario-04 parent reproduced the same combined one-minute behavior.

Authorization and malicious intent remained unproven.

## 📌 HOW

Lubaba:

1. validated current Unbound telemetry;
2. normalized trailing-dot qnames;
3. inspected raw child labels and lengths;
4. reproduced Detection v1.0 at one-minute scale;
5. validated the production alert;
6. separated query and reply semantics;
7. built the UTC timeline;
8. measured the normal baseline;
9. compared legitimate long-label traffic;
10. applied the full rule to non-Scenario-04 traffic;
11. scoped client/environment recurrence;
12. formed a human hypothesis;
13. validated AI against raw evidence;
14. locked disposition and handed the case to IR.
